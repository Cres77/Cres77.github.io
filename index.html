About me

I like hardware I can test with my hands. This past summer I was an electrical controls engineering intern at Laitram Machinery, where I wrote automated PLC test sequences that cut product testing time by 75%.

On campus I lead the electrical team for UM::Autonomy, which builds autonomous boats. I designed the boat's buck converter power system and led two teams of four that built boards to monitor the current, voltage and temperature of individual components. That work helped the team finish 4th in the world at the International RoboBoat Competition. I'm also a member of the Society of Hispanic Professional Engineers.
